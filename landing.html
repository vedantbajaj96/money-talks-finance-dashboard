What you get

Everything you need to understand your money

Connected banking you can trust

Link your bank accounts, credit cards, and investment accounts via Plaid — the most trusted bank connection used by thousands of financial apps. Takes under a minute to set up, syncs automatically from then on.

Automatic categorization that learns

Our engine organizes every transaction automatically — no rules to write. Review when you have time, fix anything with one click, and we get smarter every time you do.

Ask anything about your money

Type any financial question in plain language — "how much did I spend on restaurants last month?" or "am I saving more than last year?" — and get a direct answer backed by your actual transaction history.

Your data, encrypted and private

No ads. No selling your data. No third-party analytics. Your financial data is encrypted at rest and never shared — with anyone. This is a private tool built for people who take their finances seriously.

Things people ask before signing up

100% free. MoneyTalk is open source — you host it yourself, so there are no subscription fees. The only costs are your own API keys (Plaid for bank connections, Anthropic or Gemini for AI categorization), which you configure once and own entirely.
We use Plaid, the same bank connection technology used by thousands of financial apps including Venmo and Robinhood. The connection is read-only — MoneyTalk can see your transactions, but can't move money or make any changes.
Only you. Your data is encrypted and stored privately. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to train any models. Not even us can read your individual transactions.
Plaid supports over 12,000 US financial institutions — all major banks, credit unions, and brokerage accounts. If your bank has online banking, it almost certainly works.
Clone the repo on GitHub, add your API keys to a .env file, and run it with Docker or Python. The README walks you through the full setup — typically under 15 minutes.
